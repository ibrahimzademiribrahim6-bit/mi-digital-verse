Solo Leveling Season 2: Everything You Need to Know

⚔️

The anime phenomenon Solo Leveling is back with breathtaking Season 2 episodes featuring Sung Jinwoo's evolution into the Shadow Monarch. The first episode already shattered streaming records.

Key updates: The new season covers the return to the job-change quest, introduces iconic boss fights, and features an upgraded animation quality compared to the first season.

Japanese studio, A-1 Pictures, has confirmed a brand new soundtrack by Hiroyuki Sawano, adding epic orchestral layers to the high-octane battles.

New episode releases every Saturday on major streaming platforms. The community is already predicting the Season 3 announcement after the finale.
