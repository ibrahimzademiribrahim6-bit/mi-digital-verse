Lightning Degree: The Awakening Arc

⚡

The martial arts world is shaken as Lightning Degree enters its most explosive arc yet. The protagonist, Kang Woojin, has finally mastered the fifth level of the Thunder God Technique, unlocking powers previously thought mythical.

Fans are thrilled with the recent chapters showing intense training sequences and a complex new villain: The Storm Emperor, whose dark lightning threatens to consume the entire Murim world.

The latest chapter (Chapter 203) featured an awe-inspiring battle sequence, with fan-favorite characters joining forces. The artwork has reached cinematic quality, and many predict this will be the manhwa's defining season.

Release schedule remains steady every Tuesday and Saturday on major platforms.
